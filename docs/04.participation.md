If you are interested in sharing your expertise, supporting the structuring of the network and initiating concrete synergies between you laboratory and create news collaborations...  or if you are already part of another African network, **come to Abdijan**! 

### How to participate ?

* You can participate by sharing your experience in the form of a poster or presentation of your work, sharing also the difficulties you encounter in your everyday life.

* If you are a bioanalyst, biologist, or genomics researcher, present your scientific results and the technical challenges you face in analyzing your data. 

* If you are a server or cluster administrator, come share your struggles. If If you are completely isolated, come network.

* If you are already part of a network, come share your experience !. 


### Practical information 

*When?:* 15 - 17 December 2025

*Where?*: WAVE Regional Center of Excellence, Université Félix Houphouët-Boigny, Bingerville, Abdijan

*Contact*: bioinfo@wave-center.org


#####  Expenses 

* Travel expenses will be covered by each participant.

* Lunch will be provided during the event.
