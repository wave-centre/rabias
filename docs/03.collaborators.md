### WAVE Regional Center of Excellence

The [WAVE](https://wave-center.org/) (Central and West African Virus Epidemiology) Regional Center of Excellence is headquartered at Félix Houphouët‑Boigny University in Bingerville, Côte d’Ivoire, within the Science & Innovation Pole. Launched in 2015 and supported by the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation and the UK’s Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, WAVE was later endorsed by ECOWAS in 2021 as the Regional Center for managing transboundary plant viruses, particularly those threatening staple root and tuber crops like cassava—which feeds hundreds of millions and can lose up to 100 % of yield due to viral diseases. Its core activities include research & development, laboratory capacity building, training of MSc and PhD students across its network of national research institutions in about 10 Central and West African countries, and outreach programs—especially supporting women scientists through leadership and mentoring initiatives like the WAVE Women Career Accelerator

### Joseph Ki-Zerbo University

Located in Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso, the [Université Joseph Ki‑Zerbo](https://www.ujkz.bf/) (commonly UJKZ) is a major public university founded in 1974—originally as the University of Ouagadougou, and renamed in December 2015 in honor of historian Joseph Ki‑Zerbo. It is the oldest and largest higher education institution in Burkina Faso, serving over 30,000 students across several faculties, including humanities and social sciences, law and political science, economic sciences, sciences and technology, health sciences, and applied sciences. The university houses multiple institutes and research units—including a UNESCO Chair on Women, Gender, Science, Society and Sustainable Development—and offers master’s and doctoral programmes, including specialized training such as GIS applications, health, and environment management

### IFB (Institut Français de Bioinformatique)

The [IFB](https://www.france-bioinformatique.fr/), the Bioinformatics French Institut is a national infrastructure dedicated to bioinformatics. It provides resources and services for research in biology, with a focus on genomic and post-genomic data analysis. The IFB plays a key role in the development and dissemination of bioinformatics tools in France.


### SFBI (Société Française de Bioinformatique)

The [SFBI](https://www.sfbi.fr/) is a learned society that brings together bioinformatics professionals in France. It aims to promote research, education, and applications in bioinformatics. The SFBI organizes conferences, workshops, and training sessions to facilitate exchanges and collaboration among researchers and professionals in the field.


### Master in Bioinformatics, University of Montpellier

The [Bioinformatic Master of Montpellier University - UM](https://formations.umontpellier.fr/fr/formations/master-XB/master-bioinformatique-KKI9GZEV.html) is a high-level program that prepares students for the challenges of modern bioinformatics. This program offers solid training in biology, computer science, and mathematics, enabling students to acquire the skills needed to analyze and interpret complex biological data.


### Institut de Recherche pour le Développement

The [IRD](https://www.ird.fr/) is a French public research institution dedicated to development research. It conducts research projects in various fields, including biology, ecology, and health, in collaboration with international partners. The IRD is committed to producing scientific knowledge to address the challenges of sustainable development.


### i-Trop Platform

The [i-Trop](https://bioinfo.ird.fr/) platform is a bioinformatics facility based at the IRD center in Montpellier. It is part of the South Green platform and the French Institute of Bioinformatics (IFB). The i-Trop platform supports research projects aimed at improving the health and well-being of populations in tropical regions. It offers advanced resources and services for the analysis of biological data. The platform also provides practical training in high-performance computing (HPC) to advanced data analysis like next-generation sequencing data analysi and programming.
