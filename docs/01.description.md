### First interdisciplinary meeting of the bioinformatics community in West Africa

[WAVE Center](https://wave-center.org/), Félix Houphouët-Boigny University [UFHB](https://www.ufhb.edu.ci/), Joseph Ki-Zerbo University [UJKS](https://www.ujkz.bf/) and the French National Research Institute for Sustainable Development [IRD](https://www.ird.fr/), are organizing a meeting in West Africa for bioinformaticians, system administrators, and all professionals around these fields.

This meeting will be held in Abidjan, from **December 15 to 17, 2025**, and will bring together some **40 participants** from various countries in the region (Benin, Burkina Faso, Ivory Coast, Senegal, Mali, Niger, Ghana, Nigeria, Togo, Kenya, Cameron, DRC, South Africa) working on bioinformatics-related trades in various fields around plants and health.

The aim of this first meeting is to build up a **core of key people to structure a regional community** in bioinformatics and systems administration. To promote interdisciplinary research between biology, genomics, computer science, mathematics and statistics, and to bring together the bioinformatics community in West Africa. This need has been identified on several occasions in recent years, notably during regional training courses held in Yvory Coast and Burkina Faso. 

The establishment of a collaborative exchange framework could make possible to:

* Support better recognition and understanding of this emerging discipline and the professions related to genomics and bioinformatics (biology, mathematics, statistics, system administration);
* Develop a network and connect scientists trained in genomics and bioinformatics;
* Share available equipment and infrastructure;
* Design and implement joint projects among various stakeholders;
* Facilitate experience sharing and strengthen the capacities of different teams;
* Support the search for funding for joint projects and programs;
* Foster collaboration with other internationally organized structures.


The Bioinformatics French Institut [IFB](https://www.france-bioinformatique.fr/), the Bioinformatics French Society [SFBI](https://www.sfbi.fr/) and the [Bioinformatic Master of Montpellier University - UM](https://formations.umontpellier.fr/fr/formations/master-XB/master-bioinformatique-KKI9GZEV.html) will be on hand to share their expertise.
 
If you are interested in sharing your expertise, supporting the structuring of the network and initiating concrete synergies between these structures (IFB, SFBI, UM, WAVE, [CIBiG](https://cibig-wave.github.io/) ...) or if you are already part of another African network, **come to Abdijan**!
